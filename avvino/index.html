A Local Commitment

Tim & Janine's
table

Avvino opened in 2013 with a single conviction: that the best ingredients were already here, grown and raised by the farmers and purveyors of the Rochester region. Executive Chef Tim Caschette has honored that commitment ever since, building a menu that shifts with the seasons and surprises every visit.

Sommelier and co-owner Janine Wasley curates the wine list — nearly 200 selections — with the same care Tim brings to the kitchen. Together they've created a restaurant where the glass and the plate are equally considered.

Three dining experiences: The Main Dining Room, the intimate Meyer's Den, and The Patio when the season allows.

Executive Chef Tim Caschette & Sommelier Janine Wasley

Our Name

Avvinare

Avvino comes from the wine term Avvinare — the traditional European practice of priming stemware and decanters for proper wine service.

You pour a small sip of wine into your glass, swirl it, pour it into the glass of the person next to you, and repeat before finally discarding the sample. The glass you then serve has only the pure wine from the bottle, nothing altering its essential aroma or flavor.
