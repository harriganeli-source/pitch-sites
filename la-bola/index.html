Our Story

Your Mediterranean
Culinary Haven

A light fare restaurant and bar with Spanish & Mediterranean influences in the heart of downtown Rochester.

We fuse vibrant Spanish and Mediterranean flavors with the freshest ingredients, offering a diverse selection of thoughtfully crafted dishes and cocktails.

A proud sister restaurant of Avvino.
