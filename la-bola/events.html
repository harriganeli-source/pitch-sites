Private & Corporate Events

A venue for every occasion

Whether you're planning an intimate dinner for six or a conference for up to 600, the Mercantile on Main has the ideal space for you. Each venue can be tailored to your needs — personalized décor, custom menus from Chef Gus, and a full bar with curated wine list.

Our team and trusted partners are here to make every detail exceptional, from the first inquiry to the last toast.
