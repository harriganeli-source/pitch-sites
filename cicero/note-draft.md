Dear Ms. Cicero,

My name is Eli Harrigan. I design and build websites for professional practices in Rochester.

A firm ranked Tier 1 by Best Lawyers for fifteen straight years does not need a website to bring in work, and I will not pretend otherwise. Your clients come by reputation and referral, the way the best work always does.

But every referred client still looks once before they call. So do the sons and daughters who will one day settle the estates you planned, and decide whether the family stays with the family's attorney. That one look is the only job the website has.

I know that moment from the inside. My wife lost her mother a year and a half ago, and settling the estate has been part of our life ever since. I have seen what careful work means to a family in that year, and how rarely the firms who do it say anything about that care. Helping a firm like yours say it is the project I would most want to take on.

I sketched what that first look could be and enclosed it. It is built from what is already yours: the brackets, the teal, the words on your letterhead. Nothing about it is final.

If it interests you, I would be glad to talk. If not, please keep it with my compliments.
