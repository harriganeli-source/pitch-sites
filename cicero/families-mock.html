If you are settling a loved one’s estate,
start here.

The weeks after a death are no time to learn probate. This page is for the spouses, sons, and daughters who suddenly hold a folder of papers and no map. Here is what matters now, what can wait, and what you can hand to us.

{this week}

Order several copies of the death certificate. Find the will and leave it as you found it. Keep paying the household bills you recognize. Do not distribute money or belongings yet, even to family.

{soon}

Let statements arrive and keep them in one place. Note any automatic payments. When relatives ask what happens next, it is enough to say the estate is being handled and no decisions are made yet.

{ours to carry}

Probate filings. Notices to heirs. Creditor claims. The estate account, the tax returns, and the schedule the court expects. This is the part we do every day, so that you do not have to learn it in the hardest year.

Bring us the folder as it is. We will sort it together.
