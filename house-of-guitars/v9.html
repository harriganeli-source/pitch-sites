From Basement to Rock Royalty

You say you want a revolution…

In 1964, Beatle-mania came to America and brothers Armand and Bruce opened their first store in their mom’s basement. By day they were average Joes delivering newspapers and working at the local meat market. By night, they were plotting how they could take part in the rock revolution. As they started selling guitars out of their basement, they quickly became a destination for riff-raff to hang out. It wasn’t long before Mom had enough of the lingering smoke and loud riffing and had to kick them to the curb!

“People hear from major bands that we’re the place to go, all the national acts know about us. They trust us because of our reputation.” — Armand Schaubroeck, Owner
