What I heard

One home for both sides of your work, that feels like you and makes it easy for the right person to reach out.

You've built something real, and you want the site to reflect it. You want it clean, natural, and calm, with your own words and photography, so the right person feels something when they land and knows how to start a conversation.

You also want it to hold both sides of your work without feeling split in two. The coaching side (midlife, purpose, the next chapter) and the consulting side (fractional leadership, program management, people-first change) are one you, and the site should feel that way while making it clear which door is theirs.

The direction

A calm, unhurried site led by your own photography of the lake, with room to breathe. It carries the quiet, reflective voice of Living in the Middle.

A welcoming home page, then a focused page each for coaching and consulting, so each side of your work speaks in its own voice, one warm, one grounded. Everything points to one thing: starting a conversation. Your testimonials sit where trust belongs, and your writing gets a proper invitation. It reads like a calm, confident version of you.
