Eli Harrigan  ·  Client Agreement
Agreement between Eli Harrigan and Andrea Silveria / Silveria Coaching & Consulting LLC
Date: August 22, 2026

1. Services

Eli Harrigan agrees to provide the following services for Silveria Coaching & Consulting LLC: website design and development (a home page, a coaching page, and a consulting page), collaborative copywriting drawn from the client's own writing, testimonial sections, and an invitation to the client's newsletter. The project also includes a brand identity created from scratch: a logo and wordmark, a color palette and type system, and an email signature, applied consistently across the site. The site will be migrated cleanly from the client's current Squarespace site, keeping the client's existing email working and the site live at the same web address. The full scope is described in the accompanying itemized proposal.

2. Payment

The total project fee is $4,500, reflecting website design and development ($3,500) and brand identity ($1,000). Payment is made in two stages: $2,250 due upon signing this agreement to begin, and $2,250 due at launch, once the client is happy and the site is live. Payment may be made via the link provided or by check made out to Eli Harrigan. The collaborative process described in this agreement is unchanged by the payment schedule: the client approves the design direction before full build-out, and revisions proceed as described in Section 3.

3. Revisions

The project fee includes reasonable revisions during the design and development process. Revisions are considered reasonable when they refine the agreed-upon direction. A significant change in direction, scope, or structure after initial approval of concepts may be quoted as additional work.

4. Ongoing Work After Launch

Following the launch of the completed website, any updates, additions, or changes requested by the client will be billed at $100 per hour, in quarter-hour (.25) increments, invoiced upon completion. Minor text edits, date changes, and similar small updates will be billed at .25 increments. Structural or design changes will be subject to a one-hour minimum. A monthly maintenance arrangement is available upon request.

5. Timeline

Both parties agree to communicate actively and in good faith to keep the project moving. A schedule will be set once the project begins, and delivery depends on timely receipt of materials, content, and account access from the client. Delays caused by the client (such as slow feedback or unavailability) may affect the delivery timeline. Eli Harrigan will provide reasonable notice of any delays on his end.

6. Intellectual Property

All final deliverables — including the website, its content, and the brand identity created for this project (logo, wordmark, color palette, and type system) — become the property of Andrea Silveria / Silveria Coaching & Consulting LLC upon receipt of final payment in full. Work-in-progress materials remain the property of Eli Harrigan until that time. Eli Harrigan retains the right to display the completed work in his portfolio and promotional materials.

7. Hosting & Domain

The first year of hosting is included in the project fee. Thereafter, hosting, backups, and infrastructure upkeep are provided at $300 per year. If the site experiences significant traffic growth that materially increases hosting costs, or if the scope of the site changes significantly (e.g., added functionality, e-commerce, large media libraries), hosting costs will be discussed and agreed upon at that time. The client is responsible for registering and maintaining ownership of their own domain names, including andreasilveria.com and any additional domains pointed at the site.

The website is built in standard web code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and is fully portable. Should the client ever wish to move to a different hosting provider, the complete site files will be provided for transfer at no additional charge.

8. Cancellation

If the client cancels the project after work has begun, payment for work completed to date, valued at the hourly rate of $100/hour, is non-refundable and covers time and resources already committed. Any portion of the project fee beyond the value of work completed will be refunded.

9. Third-Party Licenses & Services

If the project requires third-party assets or services — including stock photography, licensed fonts, scheduling tools, email marketing services, or other materials — the cost of those licenses and subscriptions is the responsibility of the client and runs under the client's own accounts. Eli Harrigan will communicate any such costs in advance for approval before purchase.

10. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. Any disputes arising from this agreement will first be addressed through good-faith negotiation between both parties.

11. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep the terms of this agreement and any proprietary business information shared during the project confidential.

12. Limitation of Liability

Eli Harrigan is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from this project. No specific search engine rankings, traffic levels, or business outcomes are guaranteed. Total liability is limited to the amount paid under this agreement.

13. Entire Agreement

This agreement, together with the accompanying itemized proposal, constitutes the entire understanding between the parties. Any changes must be agreed to in writing by both parties.
