Meet Andrea

From burnout to clarity.

After more than thirty years in tech, leading engineering, business analysis, and transformation teams, I reached a moment I couldn't push through. When I was laid off from a VP role, it shook something loose.

What followed was a reckoning with how much of my identity had been built on being useful, and how little I'd asked whether the work still meant anything. I trained as a coach. I returned to writing and picked my camera back up, not as hobbies but as a way of finding out who I was without the title.

Now I do two things. I consult with organizations navigating the kind of technical and process transformation I used to lead from the inside. And I coach the leaders inside those transformations, usually somewhere in the middle of their careers, who are asking the same question I had to answer: what's this all for.

You don't have to break to begin again.
