What I heard

"Just a nice simple site. Credibility and brand bookings."

You hired a company a year or two ago and it was a disappointing experience: a WordPress site you find unintuitive, a domain they've been holding over you, and a newsletter box on the homepage that doesn't reach you. Meanwhile your social media is starting to take off again. Instagram has found its groove, TikTok is moving fast, and you repost to all three, so more people than ever are going to look you up.

What you want is super simple: an about me, a newsletter completely set up with email, a contact for bookings and brand deals, and a place that points to your social posts, your products, and your blog. Ecommerce eventually, but not now.

Three fully custom options, from $2,500 up to $9,500 for the fully produced, fully kept version with the store built in, depending on how far we take it. Each is built around you and none is off the shelf. Here's how I'd think about it for you:

How it goes

  1. The words. With Plus or Signature, an hour-long session, deeper than our texts: we work through your story, who the site is for, and what a brand should see when they land, and I take your exact words away with me to become the site's copy. With Core, I draft from what you've already posted and you mark it up. Either way, nothing else is needed from you until there's something to react to.
  2. The build. The real site, your words, on your screen. You react, I refine, and we keep going until it feels right, within the scope of the option you've chosen.
  3. Launch. I point your domain at the new site and switch the newsletter on with it, so it all goes live at the same address. Then your launch support begins.
