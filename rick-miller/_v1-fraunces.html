Therapy & Consulting

Four ways to work with Rick.

01

Couples

Couples appreciate Rick’s warm, interactive style. He is a keynote speaker at couples conferences, faculty for Esther Perel, Stan Tatkin, and Terry Real, and co-host of the Modern Couples podcast.

02

LGBTQ+

Rick was writing about coming out, sexuality, and shame decades before most clinicians would touch those topics. He continues to support LGBTQ+ people and their families.

03

Hypnosis

Instead of the scripts most clinicians use, Rick builds each session around the client’s own imagination. He teaches the approach at hypnosis conferences internationally.

04

Consulting

Clinical supervision, workplace culture assessment, and conflict resolution for companies, family businesses, and mental health agencies.

The signature work

Gay sons and their mothers.

The most significant theme of Rick’s career is the bond between gay sons and their mothers. Across his own life and his clinical work, he has seen mothers transcend cultural and religious barriers to love and accept their sons for who they are.

The work became a TEDx talk, a documentary film, and Gay Sons and Mothers, an ongoing nonprofit project.
